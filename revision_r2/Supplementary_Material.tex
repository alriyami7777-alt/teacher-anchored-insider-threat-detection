\section*{S1. Calibration and Alert Evidence (Earlier ODST Configuration)}

\textit{Method.} Secondary calibration and alert analyses belong to an earlier frozen ODST
configuration rather than the final TRACE checkpoint. Probability calibration
uses five-fold user-grouped out-of-fold temperature scaling. Alert outputs
are consolidated within users, grouping alerts on the same or consecutive
calendar days into one analytical episode. These episodes are workload
summaries rather than verified incidents.

\textit{Results.}

A frozen earlier seed-42 ODST configuration provides secondary calibration
and alert-workload evidence. User-grouped out-of-fold temperature scaling
reduced Brier score from 0.175 to 0.004 and expected calibration error from
0.413 to 0.005. At its frozen operating point, 661 sequence alerts involved
36 users and collapsed into 42 one-day-gap analytical episodes.

\smallskip
These results are not reassigned to TRACE because they were produced by an
earlier frozen configuration. Likewise, historical latency profiling is
retained only as development evidence because it used a superseded ODST
configuration. Exact final-model deployment latency is therefore not
claimed.



\section*{S2. Unseen-User Evaluation: All Views}
All models were trained on 1,500 development users and scored once on 500 held-out CERT r5.2 users over their training and validation periods (pre-registered protocol with Amendments 1 and 2). Values are three-seed mean PR-AUC. View sizes: Pooled 213,000 windows, 1,172 positive (prevalence 0.0055); Train period 197,000 windows, 917 positive (prevalence 0.0047); Validation period 16,000 windows, 255 positive (prevalence 0.0159); Offset-0 11,000 windows, 62 positive (prevalence 0.0056); Endpoint 213,000 windows, 302 positive (prevalence 0.0014).
