# Objective 3 Protocol: Robustness

**Research objective:** Evaluate controlled robustness under degraded inputs.

**Chapter 3 phase:** Phase 3, robustness assessment.

## Conditions

- Source-channel ablation.
- Continuous feature noise.
- Prediction degradation.
- Route and leaf stability.
- Cross-release portability where protocol compatibility permits.

## Limitation

Source-channel ablation is not equivalent to every operational form of missing data. This distinction must remain explicit.
